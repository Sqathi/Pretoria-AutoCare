About Pretoria AutoCare Detailing

Pretoria AutoCare Detailing is a small business that provides professional vehicle cleaning and detailing services in Pretoria.

The business was created to help customers keep their vehicles clean, presentable and well maintained without having to spend excessive time at a traditional car wash.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide convenient, reliable and affordable vehicle detailing while delivering consistent customer service.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local vehicle-care brand recognised for professional workmanship, convenient booking and customer satisfaction.

Who We Serve

Our services are intended for a range of customers who need reliable vehicle cleaning and detailing.

  • Private vehicle owners
  • Students
  • Working professionals
  • Families
  • Small businesses
